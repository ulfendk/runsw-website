Privacy policy for Miniature Brigade: Board Games

Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version

Miniature Brigade: Board Games (in Danish: Myresoldater: Brætspil) does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The game is made by RU Nielsen Software (runsw) in Denmark. This policy covers the app on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac and Android, including Android TV.

Your progress stays on your device. If you buy the full game, Apple or Google handle the payment. If you're signed in to Game Center, the achievements you earn are reported to Apple.

What stays on your device

The game keeps a few things on your device so they're there the next time you play. None of it is sent to us.

Kept on the deviceWhy
Settings: language, graphics quality, sound effects, read-aloud, mini-games on or offSo the game starts the way you left it
Whether the tutorial is doneSo it isn't shown again
Which lines have been read aloudEach line is read the first three times, then only shown
Your own formation for capture the flagSo you can play it again
Achievements you've earnedTo show them in the game
How many free games have been playedThe free version includes 10 games against the easy computer
Whether the full game has been boughtSo it works offline, before the store answers

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Buying the full game

The full game is a single in-app purchase. The App Store or Google Play handles the payment, under their own privacy policies: Apple and Google.

We never see your name, email, card or Apple ID / Google account. The store only tells the game whether the full game is bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on another device or after a reinstall.

The purchase screen sits behind a parental gate, so a child can't open it by accident.

Game Center achievements

On iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac, the game reports achievements to Apple's Game Center when you're signed in to it. Apple stores them with your Game Center account and may show them to your Game Center friends, as your Game Center settings allow. We can't see who earned them.

If you're not signed in, or you turn Game Center off in Settings, nothing is reported, and the achievements stay on the device. The Android version doesn't report achievements anywhere yet. If we add Google Play Games, we'll update this policy first.

Software from others in the game

The game is built with the Unity engine. It uses Unity's in-app purchasing library only to talk to the App Store or Google Play.

Unity Analytics, crash reporting and all other Unity online services are turned off. The game includes no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it never asks for your advertising identifier.

The game doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. It only goes online to reach the store and Game Center.

Children

The game is made for children to play, and we collect nothing from them or anyone else.

  • No ads, and no links that lead out of the game, except the store's own purchase screen behind the parental gate.
  • No chat, no messages and nothing a child can share with other players.
  • No accounts and no sign-up. Game Center is Apple's, and its use is set by the parent in Screen Time.

This is how we meet COPPA in the US and the GDPR's rules for children in the EU.

Your rights, changes and contact

Under the GDPR you have the right to see, correct and delete personal data about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete. To remove what the game keeps on your device, delete the app. Game Center data is managed by Apple, under your Apple ID's privacy settings.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts collecting anything, we'll say so here before the update reaches you.

Questions: write to RU Nielsen Software at games@runsw.dk. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.
